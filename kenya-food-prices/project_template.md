Analysis of Food Price Trends in Kenya

A Study of Maize and Bean Prices Across Markets

1. What is the problem you are solving?

Food prices affect the cost of living in Kenya. Changes in maize and bean prices can make it difficult for households to plan their budgets and afford essential foods. Prices may also differ across markets and over time.

This project analyzes historical maize and bean prices in Kenya to understand price trends, differences between markets, and possible seasonal patterns.

2. What tools did you use?

* Python: The main programming language used for the analysis.
* Pandas: Used to load, clean, organize, and analyze the food price dataset.
* NumPy: Used to support numerical calculations.
* Matplotlib: Used to create graphs showing price trends and comparisons.
* Jupyter Notebook: Used to organize and present the code, analysis, and visualizations.

The project uses real historical food price data for Kenya, covering the available records from 2007 to October 2026.

3. What insights did you discover, or what solutions do you want to offer?

The project investigates how maize and bean prices change over time, how their prices compare, how average prices differ across markets, and whether monthly patterns may exist.

The analysis uses yearly averages, market comparisons, summary statistics, and monthly averages to identify patterns that could help people better understand food price changes.

The aim is to provide useful evidence for monitoring food prices and identifying periods or markets that may require closer attention.

4. How would a business or community benefit from your work?

* Households: Better understanding of historical price patterns can support food budgeting and purchasing decisions.
* Traders and retailers: Market comparisons can help them understand price differences when planning where and when to buy or sell.
* Farmers: Historical price information can provide context when planning production and market activities.
* Researchers and policymakers: The analysis can support discussions about food affordability and the need for more complete food price data.

Overall, the project demonstrates how Python data analysis can turn historical food price data into information that supports better-informed decisions.
